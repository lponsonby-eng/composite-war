# Building credits

Selected buildings from these free packs, cropped into `atlas.png` by `tools/sprites/buildings.py`.
The game recolours each region's buildings to its tone and to the game's palette.

| Pack | Artist | Terms (from the pack page / readme) |
|---|---|---|
| [Medieval Buildings Pixel Art Pack](https://devs-in-exile.itch.io/medieval-buildings-pixel-art-pack) | Devs in Exile | free download; no licence text found on the page |
| [Tiny Top-Down Village](https://jedimeisterx.itch.io/tiny-top-down-village-58-cute-80x80-pixel-buildings) | Pixel-Deck | use in free or paid games, modify and recolour; do not resell or redistribute the complete pack unchanged |
| [Ruined Village Buildings](https://mutterpixel-studio.itch.io/ruined-village-buildings-pixel-art-assets) | MutterPixel Studio | use in games, may be edited; no resale, no NFT or AI training, raw files only within a project |

The repository should be private while these are in it (the last two packs do not allow sharing the files on their own).
